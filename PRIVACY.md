# Privacy Policy

LiveClearMic is designed to work locally on your Android device.

## Data collection

LiveClearMic does not collect, store, sell or transmit personal data.

## Audio

LiveClearMic does not record, store or upload microphone audio.

The app only reacts to supported microphone activity in order to manage the Bluetooth HFP audio route.

## Local settings

App settings, such as the selected Bluetooth headset and Startmute configuration, are stored locally on the device.

## Analytics and advertising

LiveClearMic does not use analytics, tracking or advertising services.

## Network access

LiveClearMic does not require internet access for its core functionality.

## Contact

For bugs or technical issues, please use GitHub Issues.
